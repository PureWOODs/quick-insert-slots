Drop .bwpreset files here to give slots their presets.

Bitwig saves a device, a chain or an FX Grid patch as a .bwpreset from its device menu
("Save Preset…"), or from the device's context menu in the Browser.

They fill slots 1…64 in file-name order: 01-bass.bwpreset fills slot 1, 02-lead.bwpreset slot 2,
and so on. Settings win over files in this folder.
